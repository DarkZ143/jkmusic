Rasika Guidelines

Baithak Etiquette for Listening Connoisseurs

To preserve the meditative tranquility of live classical sound, we invite our guests to observe the sacred conventions of Indian baithaks.

do_not_disturb_on

Silence & Stillness

Mobile phones must remain on silent mode. We kindly ask patrons to avoid entry or exit during a raga's unfolding Alap phase.

pan_tool

Daad & Appreciation

Vocal appreciation ('Wah', 'Kiya Baat Hai') and nodding in taal rhythm are warmly welcomed at the arrival of the Sam beat.

nature_people

Footwear & Attire

Traditional Indian or comfortable smart attire is encouraged. Footwear is kept at the auditorium entrance before stepping onto carpeted riyaz mats.

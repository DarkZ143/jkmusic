Yes. JK Music Club is an accredited center affiliated with Akhil Bharatiya Gandharva Mahavidyalaya Mandal, India's foremost classical music governing board established in 1901. Our curricula prepare students for Prarambhik, Praveshika, Madhyama, and Visharad (equivalent to a Bachelor's Degree in Classical Music), recognized by universities worldwide for academic and cultural credits.
Online trials occur via our high-fidelity stereo audio stream portal (optimized for low-latency acoustic instruments). A senior faculty member will conduct the vocal pitch or finger-dexterity evaluation identically to an in-studio session, providing real-time feedback on breathing and posture.

## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the timeless reverence of Indian classical music (*Gurukul Parampara*) merged seamlessly with the precision, clarity, and accessibility of a world-class contemporary performing arts academy. The aesthetic language reconciles classical riyaz culture with modern digital learning pedagogy.

### Visual Style
- **Aesthetic Direction**: Neo-Classical Indian Editorial. A refined hybrid of classical Indian cultural cues and contemporary, high-whitespace digital layout.
- **Atmospheric Expression**: Tactile warmth inspired by aged sandalwood instruments, glowing brass tanpura hardware, natural parchment notation sheets, and deep midnight performance halls.
- **Motif Integration**: Linear geometric motifs evoking tanpura strings, micro-ornamental gold framing inspired by *jali* lattices, and typographical nods to *Swaras* (Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni). Decorative elements remain structural, architectural, and restrained—never crowded or kitsch.
- **Emotional Intent**: Reverence, mastery, artistic devotion, calm discipline, and premium scholastic distinction.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classical Indian symbology and performance arts:

- **Primary (`#991B1B` - Sindoor Vermilion Crimson)**: Signifies devotion (*Bhakti*), focus, and classical tradition. Used for core brand touchpoints, authoritative hero calls to action, active navigation states, and mastery progress markers.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Kesari Marigold Gold)**: Embodies celebration, auspicious beginnings, and spiritual illumination. Employed for highlight states, badges, ratings, key micro-interactions, decorative rules, and golden glows.
- **Tertiary (`#1E1B4B` - Midnight Peacock Indigo)**: Represents the introspective depth of midnight ragas and royal court patronage. Utilized for deep structural containers, high-contrast badges, dark-mode surfaces, and prominent display text.
- **Neutral (`#FDFBF7` - Sandalwood Ivory)**: A warm, tactile canvas evoking hand-pressed parchment and seasoned wood, creating comfortable contrast without the sterility of pure white.

### Functional Application
- Canvas backgrounds default to Sandalwood Ivory (`#FDFBF7`), with secondary cards layered in `#F5EBE1` and highlight panels in `#FEF3C7`.
- Deep contrast cards (e.g., Masterclass showcases, evening Raga modules) invert the palette using `#1E1B4B` as the container background with `#D97706` gold framing and Ivory text.
- Critical text contrast adheres strictly to WCAG AA/AAA standards by setting headlines in Deep Indigo (`#0F172A` / `#1E1B4B`) against ivory fields.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical poetics with clean, legible information architecture:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Imparts an archival, royal, and literary cadence reminiscent of classical concert handbills and ancient treatises (*Natya Shastra*). Used for high-impact section titles, student achievements, and raga course titles.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric purity and warm humanism, optimizing readability for lesson breakdowns, practice logs, notations, schedules, and digital riyaz tools.
- **Ornamental Text Usage**: Subtitles and tala/swara markers utilize `label-sm` or `label-md` with extended letter-spacing (uppercase tracking) accompanied by fine hairline dividers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured on a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to evoke rhythmic balance (*Taal*):

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, 3rem canvas outer margins with a maximum container boundary of 1280px.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, 2rem margins.
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, 1rem gutters, 1.25rem margins.
- **Rhythmic Cadence**: Layouts favor asymmetrical breathing room around core hero cards, allowing imagery of Indian instruments and maestro profiles to breathe. Vertical rhythm relies on multi-scale multiples of `space-md` (16px) and `space-xl` (40px) to balance scholarly dense curricula with expansive concert showcases.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than conventional harsh drop shadows, this design system builds depth through warm luminous layering and metallic boundary lines:

- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is created by placing warm ivory containers (`#FFFFFF` or `#FDFBF7`) on top of soft sandalwood canvas foundations (`#F5EBE1`), framed with delicate gold borders (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)`).
- **Amber & Vermilion Ambient Halos**: Floating interactive elements utilize low-opacity, high-spread warm glows instead of neutral gray shadows: `0 8px 30px rgba(153, 27, 27, 0.08)` or `0 10px 24px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)`.
- **Performance Stage Elevation (Deep Indigo)**: Flagship event and practice cards use dark indigo surfaces (`#1E1B4B`) with interior gold hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`), creating an illuminated, auditorium-like staging area.

## Shapes

The design system uses soft, restrained corner treatments (`0.25rem` base, `0.5rem` for cards/panels) reflecting traditional parchment folios and structural Indian architectural lintels rather than overly bubbly app interfaces.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem`)**: Applied to buttons, inputs, tags, and small utility badges.
- **Card Radius (`0.5rem`)**: Applied to curriculum modules, course cards, and modal dialogs.
- **Accent Shapes**: Key visual headers and featured maestro cards may incorporate architectural arch profiles (bracketed corners or subtle top-edge domes) combined with precise double-rule gold border lines (0.5px and 1px intervals).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Vermilion Riyaz)**: Crimson background (`#991B1B`), crisp ivory text (`#FDFBF7`), subtle gold inset highlight on hover (`0 0 0 1px #F59E0B`), 0.25rem border-radius.
- **Secondary (Sandalwood Gold)**: Warm ivory surface, 1px solid border in Kesari Gold (`#D97706`), deep indigo text (`#1E1B4B`). On hover, fills with pale marigold cream (`#FEF3C7`).
- **Tertiary / Link**: Deep indigo text, underlined with a 1.5px gold rule placed 4px below the baseline; transitions to crimson on hover.

### Cards
- **Curriculum & Lesson Card**: Sandalwood ivory background, 1px perimeter border in light gold (`#E5D5BA`), soft 0.5rem radius. Top metadata features swara or raga category in a micro gold pill.
- **Masterclass / Guru Stage Card**: Midnight Indigo background (`#1E1B4B`), warm ivory typography, framed with dual-hairline gold accents (`#F59E0B` at 30% opacity). Hover state elevates with a soft amber ambient glow.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background in natural ivory (`#FFFFFF` with `#FDFBF7` canvas).
- Border: 1px solid `#D8C7B5`. Focus state activates a sharp 1.5px crimson ring (`#991B1B`) with a faint kesari aura (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`).
- Labels are set in `label-md` Plus Jakarta Sans, positioned strictly above the input for academic clarity.

### Chips & Badges
- **Gharana / Style Chips**: Light amber tint (`#FEF3C7`), text in dark kesari (`#B45309`), rounded-sm, 1px solid border (`#FDE68A`).
- **Tala / Swara Pill**: Deep Indigo fill (`#1E1B4B`), gold text (`#F59E0B`), compact height (24px) for notation tags.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square (checkbox) or circular (radio) with 1.5px crimson borders. When checked, filled with crimson (`#991B1B`) featuring an ivory check or dot symbol.

### Specialized Academy Components
- **Swara Scale Bar**: A horizontal pitch and octave tracker featuring 7 steps (Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni) with vermilion active-state indicators and brass-colored timeline lines.
- **Tanpura Drone Tuning Bar**: Audio player strip styled like an aged teak wood board with micro gold control knobs and indigo wave monitors.
